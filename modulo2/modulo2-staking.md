# Participación y Staking en Ethereum

Ethereum transitioned to Proof of Stake (PoS) in 2022. Instead of mining, blocks are now created and secured by **validators** who participate by staking ETH.

This system is more energy-efficient and opens participation to anyone willing to lock up ETH and help secure the network.

---

## 💎 What is staking?

**Staking** means depositing ETH to activate validator software. Validators propose and attest to blocks in exchange for rewards.

- 🔐 Requires locking ETH in a smart contract  
- 🎯 Helps validate transactions and secure Ethereum  
- 🎁 Earns ETH rewards over time  
- ⚠️ Includes risk of slashing (penalty for misbehavior)

---

## 🧪 Why is staking important?

- It secures the network  
- Replaces energy-intensive mining  
- Encourages long-term alignment between users and Ethereum  
- Ensures decentralization and censorship resistance

---

## 🛠️ Ways to stake ETH

| Method                    | Description                                                |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Solo staking**          | Run your own validator (requires 32 ETH and technical setup) |
| **Staking as a service**  | Pay a provider to manage the setup for you                |
| **Staking pools**         | Combine ETH with others (e.g. Rocket Pool, Lido)           |
| **Centralized exchanges** | Stake via Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, etc.                  |

> 🔒 All methods involve locking your ETH. Unstaking is possible after recent upgrades like **Shanghai**.

---

## 📊 Staking rewards

- Rewards vary based on total ETH staked and network conditions  
- Penalties apply for going offline or attempting attacks  
- Validators earn tips (priority fees) and block rewards

You can track validator performance and network stats at:
- [https://beaconcha.in](https://beaconcha.in)
- [https://validatorqueue.com](https://validatorqueue.com)

---

## 🧩 Staking and participation

Staking is one way to **participate** actively in Ethereum’s ecosystem. Others include:

- Running a node
- Joining a DAO
- Voting in on-chain governance
- Contributing to public goods or education

---

### 💧 Liquid staking

Protocols like Lido issue **staking derivatives** (e.g., stETH) which represent your staked ETH. This allows you to earn staking rewards while retaining liquidity for DeFi usage.
