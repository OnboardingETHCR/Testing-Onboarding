# Gobernanza en Ethereum

Ethereum is a decentralized protocol, but it still needs to evolve, through upgrades, standards, and collective decisions. This is where **governance** comes in.

Ethereum’s governance is unique: it’s transparent, open to anyone, and community-driven.

---

## 🧠 What is governance?

Governance is how changes are made to Ethereum. It involves:

- Proposing and discussing improvements  
- Reaching rough consensus  
- Implementing upgrades in clients and nodes

Unlike traditional organizations, Ethereum has no central authority, decisions emerge from collaboration.

---

## 📜 Ethereum Improvement Proposals (EIPs)

EIPs are the primary way technical changes are introduced.

Each EIP includes:
- A clear proposal for change
- Rationale and potential risks
- Code specification

EIPs go through stages:
1. Draft
2. Review
3. Last Call
4. Final

> Example: EIP-1559 introduced a new gas fee mechanism.

Learn more: [https://eips.ethereum.org](https://eips.ethereum.org)

---

## 🧑‍💻 Who participates?

Governance is open to everyone:
- **Core developers** write and review EIPs
- **Node operators** choose whether to adopt changes
- **Researchers** test and validate assumptions
- **Users and community members** provide feedback

> 💬 Discussions often happen on forums like [Ethereum Magicians](https://ethereum-magicians.org), Discord, or GitHub.

---

## 🏛 DAOs and on-chain governance

While Ethereum's core protocol uses off-chain governance, many applications on Ethereum use **DAOs** (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations) for decision-making.

In a DAO:
- Members vote using tokens
- Funds are managed by smart contracts
- Decisions are transparent and enforceable

> DAOs are a powerful experiment in decentralized governance.

---

## 🌱 Ongoing evolution

Ethereum is always changing through major upgrades like:

- The Merge (PoS)
- Shanghai (withdrawals)
- Dencun (data sharding and rollup support)

And future proposals like:
- Account abstraction (EIP-4337)
- Verkle trees (better storage)
- Stateless clients
